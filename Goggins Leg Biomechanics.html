Model and assumptions

Sine-wave spring-mass model after Morin et al. (2005). Peak vertical force F = m·g·(π/2)·(flight time ÷ contact time + 1). Contact time falls with speed (my assumed fit: 0.40 − 0.035·v seconds, never longer than a step). Step time = 60 ÷ cadence. Leg length = 0.53 × height. Leg stiffness = F ÷ leg compression, vertical stiffness = F ÷ centre-of-mass drop. Speed is moving speed: his race time minus assumed stoppages (70% moving at 200+ miles, 92% at 100 to 135 miles, 97% at 50 to 100, 100% under 50). Height and weight are public estimates (6 ft 1 in and 200 lb on Wikipedia; other sites say 6 ft 2 in and 190 lb). Fatigue, downhill braking, terrain and shoes are not modelled, so real forces would differ, especially on mountain courses.
